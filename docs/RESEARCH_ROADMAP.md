# Research roadmap

**Current direction, 31 October 2026:** independent computational research into the architecture of money and institutions. The broader programme continues; Zero-Net is one test case and is not a business being launched.

1. **Real-data evaluation.** Preregister Experiment 002 before receiving a consented, de-identified invoice network. Define the data protocol, privacy controls, baseline algorithms, uncertainty analysis and falsification criteria. Measure circularity, hub concentration and simultaneous outstanding obligations. The original real-data thresholds (<3% abandon the studied mechanism configuration; ≥10% further investigation) remain proposed gates, not newly observed findings.
2. **External validity and participation.** Test whether synthetic clearability survives incomplete disclosure, disputes, timing mismatches, network boundaries and strategic refusal. Registration-gated public transaction networks are another possible validation source, subject to their own permissions and licences.
3. **Prediction.** Extend and test clearability predictors out of sample. The original H4 threshold was not met; no successful predictor is claimed.
4. **Institutional design space.** Investigate ownership, enforceability, participation and distribution jointly, using the nine-dimensional representation as a working ontology. Separate controllable rules from emergent properties and explicit assumptions from empirical measurements.
5. **Constrained AI-assisted search.** Only after a domain has defensible evaluation data, compare searched mechanisms against exact/simple baselines in held-out environments. No superior currency or autonomous discovery capability has yet been established.

The original selection, hypotheses, proofs, parameter choices and negative results remain in the historical research documents. Their commercial/pilot discussion is historical, not an active roadmap. Additional private branch investigations are preserved but require a separate publication/rights review before inclusion.
